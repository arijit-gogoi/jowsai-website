JOWSAI brings together the natural character of Eri silk and the enduring handloom traditions of Northeast India, with every piece created in Assam.

Each piece is individually handwoven, carrying the subtle variations, texture and warmth that can only come from a natural fibre and the human hand.

## Born of Silk. Shaped by Hands.

Our journey begins with carefully sourced Eri silk cocoons.

The delicate fibres are skilfully retrieved and prepared, a process requiring patience, precision and generations of specialised knowledge. The fibres are then transformed into yarn through traditional methods before being woven by hand on looms in Assam.

From fibre to finished textile, every stage is shaped by the artisan.

There is no rush in this process. Only time, skill and attention.

## The Art of Assam's Handloom

JOWSAI celebrates the rich handloom traditions of Assam and the wider cultural heritage of Northeast India, along with the generations of craftsmanship that have kept them alive.

Our designs are inspired by the natural world and the cultural landscape of Assam, translated into contemporary textiles while preserving the character and authenticity of traditional handwoven craft.

Every stole is more than a design.

It is a meeting of place, people and craft.

## A Beauty That Evolves

Eri silk possesses a rare quality: it becomes more beautiful with time.

As it is worn and carefully maintained, the fibre can develop greater softness and a distinctive natural lustre. Its texture gradually acquires a character that cannot be replicated by mass production.

Its story continues long after it leaves the loom.

The longer it is cherished, the more uniquely it becomes yours.

## Craft That Sustains Communities

Behind every JOWSAI stole is a chain of skilled craftsmanship.

From cocoon processing and fibre preparation to spinning, yarn preparation, weaving and finishing, each stage involves specialised knowledge and skilled hands.

Eri silk production therefore represents more than a textile tradition. It supports livelihoods while helping preserve the skills and knowledge that form an important part of Assam's living handloom heritage and Northeast India's rich cultural identity.

JOWSAI seeks to bring this exceptional craft from Assam and Northeast India to discerning customers around the world.

## From Assam. Made by Hand.

We believe true luxury is not defined by excess.

It is found in provenance, craftsmanship, natural materials and time.

A JOWSAI stole carries all four.

Handwoven Eri silk from Assam, Northeast India— created slowly, cherished for generations.
