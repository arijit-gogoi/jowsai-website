Eri Silk Stole — Woven Heritage, Naturally Beautiful

Discover the timeless beauty of Eri silk, brought to life through traditional handloom craftsmanship. This exquisite stole is completely handwoven by skilled artisans, preserving the character, texture and artistry of a textile made by hand.

The stole is created entirely without chemical dyes, allowing the natural character of the fibres and colours to shine through. Its intricate motifs are inspired by the Bodo tribal tradition, reflecting the community's deep relationship with nature, the environment and the world around them. Each geometric form and repeated pattern carries the visual language of a heritage passed from one generation to another.

Naturally Comfortable Throughout the Year

Eri silk is valued for its remarkable natural comfort. Its breathable character makes it pleasant to wear in hot weather, helping provide a cool and comfortable feel, while its insulating properties provide warmth during colder weather.

With time and proper care, Eri silk can develop an even more beautiful natural glaze and lustre. Rather than losing its character with age, the fabric can acquire a richer, more refined appearance, making it a textile to treasure for years.

A Piece of Bodo Heritage

More than an accessory, this stole represents a connection between craft, culture and nature. The Bodo-inspired designs celebrate the relationship between people and their natural surroundings, translated into intricate woven patterns by the hands of artisans.

Each piece may carry subtle variations in texture, colour and weave—natural characteristics of a genuinely handcrafted textile.

Care Instructions

Dry clean only.
To preserve the silk fibres, natural colours, texture and developing lustre, do not machine wash, hand wash, soak, bleach or use harsh chemical treatments. Store clean and dry, preferably folded in a breathable fabric bag away from direct sunlight and moisture.
